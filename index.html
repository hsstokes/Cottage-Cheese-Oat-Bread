Cottage Cheese Oat Bread

Oven version, adapted from bread machine v3.0. With olives, sun-dried tomato and cheddar folded through. Better crumb control than the machine cycle — check with a skewer, not the clock.

180°C fan 900g loaf tin ~2.5 hrs total 10 slices
01 Mix 15 min
02 Rise 45 min
03 Bake 50–60 min
04 Cool 2+ hrs

Ingredients

  • GF oat flour300g
  • Cottage cheese, blended smooth300g
  • Large eggs, room temp2
  • Olive oil (optional — see note)1 tbsp
  • Honey or sugar1 tbsp
  • Salt1 tsp
  • Instant yeast2 tsp
  • Xanthan gum1 tsp
  • Baking powder1 tsp
  • Warm water70ml
  • Seeds, for topping2 tbsp
  • Olives, chopped, patted dry70g
  • Sun-dried tomatoes, drained, chopped60g
  • Cheddar, grated (reserve some)80g

Water & oil note — reduced from the plain version since olives, sun-dried tomatoes and cheddar all bring their own oil and moisture. Pat olives and tomatoes dry before folding in.

Method

  1. Prep
    Eggs out of the fridge 30 minutes ahead. Preheat oven to 180°C fan. Grease and line a 900g loaf tin.
  2. Wet mix-ins
    Blend cottage cheese until completely smooth. Pat olives and sun-dried tomatoes dry with kitchen paper.
  3. Wet ingredients
    Whisk together warm water, eggs, blended cottage cheese, oil and honey until combined.
  4. Dry ingredients
    Mix oat flour, xanthan gum, baking powder and salt, then stir in the yeast.
  5. Bring together
    Fold dry into wet until just combined — thick, sticky batter. Fold in olives, sun-dried tomatoes and most of the cheddar (reserve a little). Add a spoon of oat flour if it's looser than thick cake batter — don't chase it with more water.
  6. Into the tin
    Scrape into the tin, smooth the top with a wet spatula. Scatter over seeds and reserved cheddar.
  7. Rise
    Cover loosely with a damp tea towel or oiled cling film. Leave somewhere warm until visibly puffed.
    ⏱ ~45 min
  8. Bake
    180°C fan, 50–60 minutes. Tent loosely with foil after 25–30 minutes once golden. Done when a skewer in the centre comes out clean.
    ⏱ 50–60 min
  9. Cool completely
    Turn out onto a wire rack. Cool fully before slicing — the crumb keeps setting as it cools, especially with these mix-ins.
    ⏱ 2+ hrs
