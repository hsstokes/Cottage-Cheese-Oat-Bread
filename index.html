Cottage Cheese Oat Burger Baps

Burger-bap version of the oat bread — part oat flour, part GF white bread flour for a lighter crumb, with milled flaxseed. Baked in a shallow 6-cup Yorkshire pudding tray so they cook right through, with no gummy centre.

180°C fan 6-cup Yorkshire pudding tray ~1 hr incl. cooling 6 baps
01 Mix 10 min
— Rise no wait
02 Bake 22–25 min
03 Cool 30 min

Ingredients

  • GF oat flour150g
  • GF white bread flour (plain, not self-raising)75g
  • Milled flaxseed1½ tbsp
  • Cottage cheese, blended smooth150g
  • Large eggs, room temp2
  • Olive oil1 tbsp
  • Honey or sugar2 tsp
  • Salt¾ tsp
  • Baking powder1½ tsp
  • Water, room temperature~120ml
  • Milk or beaten egg, to brush1 tbsp
  • Mixed seeds, to top2–3 tbsp
Tested — first batch baked through with an even crumb and no gummy middle. Bread flour used: Doves Farm Freee White Bread Flour Blend (rice, tapioca, potato, xanthan gum, no raising agent). The batter needed about 120ml water in total — more than first expected, as the flaxseed and bread flour soak it up.

Method

  1. Prep
    Eggs out of the fridge 30 minutes ahead. Preheat oven to 180°C fan. Grease each cup well, even though the tray is non-stick.
  2. Wet ingredients
    Blend cottage cheese until completely smooth. Stir in eggs, oil, honey and 60ml of the water with a fork until even.
  3. Dry ingredients
    Mix oat flour, bread flour, flaxseed, baking powder and salt together.
  4. Bring together
    Stir dry into wet with a spoon. It will look crumbly at first — add the remaining water a tablespoon at a time until it's a soft, sticky batter that drops slowly off the spoon. Slightly wet is better than dry.
  5. Into the tray — straight away
    Weigh about 95g into each cup so they're even. Smooth the tops with a wet spoon, brush with milk or egg, and press on the seeds.
    baking powder starts reacting on contact with liquid — don't let the batter sit
  6. Bake
    Middle shelf, 180°C fan. Turn the tray round at 12 minutes, as the back of the oven runs hotter. Check at 22 minutes: done when golden, firm, pulled away from the cups and a skewer comes out clean. Give 3–5 more minutes if not.
    ⏱ 22–25 min
  7. Cool
    Turn out of the tray straight away so the bases don't steam. Cool completely before slicing — the crumb keeps setting as it cools.
    ⏱ 30 min

Notes

  • For burgers: slice across the middle and toast the cut sides so they hold up to juices.
  • Bigger baps: up to ~115g per cup and bake 25–28 minutes. Much more and the batter may spill over the shallow cups.
  • Double batch (12): mix all dry in one bowl and all wet in another, then combine half at a time — bake the first six, keep the remaining wet mix in the fridge, and mix the second half once the tray is free.
  • Freezing: slice before freezing and toast straight from frozen.
