Greek Yoghurt Oat Burger Baps

Gluten-free burger baps made with oat flour, Greek yoghurt and psyllium husk. The yoghurt reacts with a little bicarbonate of soda for lift, and the psyllium gives structure, so they rise into domed baps with an even, baked-through crumb. Baked in a shallow Yorkshire pudding tray.

190°C fan 6-cup Yorkshire pudding tray ~1 hr 15 incl. cooling 4 large baps
01Mix10 min
02Rest10 min
03Bake~30 min
04Cool30 min

Ingredients

  • GF oat flour150g
  • GF white bread flour (plain, not self-raising)75g
  • Psyllium husk powder (unflavoured)10g
  • Milled flaxseed1 tbsp
  • Strained Greek yoghurt, plain, full-fat150g
  • Large eggs, room temp2
  • Olive oil1 tbsp
  • Honey or sugar2 tsp
  • Salt¾ tsp
  • Baking powder1½ tsp
  • Bicarbonate of soda¼ tsp
  • Water, room temperature30ml
  • Milk or beaten egg, to brush1 tbsp
  • Mixed seeds, to top2–3 tbsp

Method

  1. Prep
    Eggs and yoghurt out of the fridge 30 minutes ahead. Preheat oven to 190°C fan. Grease 4 cups well, even though the tray is non-stick.
  2. Wet ingredients
    Whisk the yoghurt, eggs, oil, honey and 30ml water with an electric hand whisk until smooth.
  3. Bring together, then rest
    Add the oat flour, bread flour, psyllium, flaxseed, baking powder, bicarb and salt on top of the wet ingredients, then whisk it all together with the electric whisk until there are no dry patches. Leave for 10 minutes — it firms up into a soft, sticky dough that holds its shape. If it's still crumbly after resting, add water a tablespoon at a time.
    bicarb reacts with the yoghurt straight away — rest for 10 minutes, no longer
  4. Shape into the tray
    Use a spatula to gather the dough into one ball, turn it out onto a board, and divide into 4 (about 140g each). Shape each into a ball and press gently into the cups. Brush with milk or egg, and press on the seeds.
  5. Bake
    Middle shelf, 190°C fan. Keep the door shut for the first 20 minutes so they don't sink. Check at 30 minutes: done when the centre reads 96–98°C on a probe thermometer, or they sound hollow when tapped underneath. A clean skewer isn't reliable with GF crumb.
    ⏱ ~30 min
  6. Cool
    Lift out of the tray once the bases are dry and set. Cool completely before slicing — the crumb keeps setting as it cools.
    ⏱ 30 min

Inside and out

Notes

  • Yoghurt: use strained Greek yoghurt. "Greek-style" is often thickened rather than strained and is runnier — if using it, leave out the water.
  • Psyllium: use the powder, not whole husks, and avoid flavoured fibre sachets. Some brands turn bakes slightly grey or purple — harmless.
  • Smaller baps: divide into 6 (about 95g each) and check at 25 minutes.
  • For burgers: slice across the middle and toast the cut sides so they hold up to juices.
  • Freezing: slice before freezing and toast straight from frozen.
